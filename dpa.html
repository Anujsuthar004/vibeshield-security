DPA

Data Processing Agreement (Article 28 GDPR).

Effective on workspace creation. Counterparty: VibeShield Security (processor). Customer is the controller for any personal data submitted into VibeShield.

1. Subject matter and duration

VibeShield processes scan inputs and account metadata on the customer's instructions in order to deliver static security analysis. Processing continues for the lifetime of the workspace and ends with workspace deletion.

2. Nature and purpose

Read-only static analysis of source code and dependency manifests. No automated decision-making with legal effect, no profiling of data subjects.

3. Categories of data subjects

  • Customer's developers and operators (account holders).
  • Any data subjects whose identifiers happen to appear in submitted source code (we treat all such occurrences as accidental and redact secret-like patterns).

4. Categories of personal data

  • Identification: email, workspace name, hashed password, session tokens.
  • Technical: API key hashes, IP-derived rate limit buckets (kept < 60 seconds).
  • Content: anything the customer chooses to paste or submit. Source files are not retained post-scan.

5. Confidentiality

All personnel authorized to access customer data are bound by written confidentiality obligations and operate under least-privilege access controls.

6. Security measures

TLS 1.2+ in transit. AES-256 at rest via managed Postgres. Bcrypt for passwords, SHA-256 for API keys. Audit logging of every state-changing action. Webhook signature verification. CSP, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy at the edge. Ephemeral worker directories with explicit cleanup. Annual review of access rights.

7. Subprocessors

  • Vercel Inc. (United States) — hosting and edge.
  • Managed Postgres provider (region selected by the customer) — Neon, Supabase, or Vercel Postgres depending on DATABASE_URL.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States) — source retrieval.
  • osv.dev (Google LLC) (United States) — public CVE lookups.
  • SMTP provider chosen by the customer — outbound email when SMTP_HOST is configured.

We notify customers at least 30 days before adding a new subprocessor and offer a right to object before activation.

8. International transfers

Where personal data is transferred outside the EEA/UK, transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (2021/914) and the UK Addendum to the SCCs, as applicable. Supplementary measures include TLS-only transport and encrypted backups.

9. Assistance to the controller

We assist with data subject requests (access, rectification, erasure, portability) within 30 days. We support DPIAs by providing this DPA, the privacy notice, and security documentation.

10. Breach notification

We notify the customer without undue delay and at the latest within 72 hours after becoming aware of a personal data breach, including the nature, categories of data, and remediation steps.

11. Return and deletion

On workspace deletion or contract termination, we delete personal data within 30 days unless retention is required by law. Backups are purged on a 35-day rolling cycle.

12. Audit

Customers may request a written audit response once per 12 months. We do not currently hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification; an external attestation roadmap will be published when a third-party engagement is contracted. We will not claim certifications we have not earned.
